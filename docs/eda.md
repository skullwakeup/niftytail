## 5. Exceedances and extremal index

## 7. Limitations

- Ljung–Box on raw returns is size-distorted under conditional heteroskedasticity, so the r rows are interpreted cautiously (methodology §9).
- Inter-exceedance times are discrete. The Exp(1) QQ reference is a continuous approximation of a discrete (geometric) null.
- θ̂ is unreliable at small N_u and is reported without a confidence interval.
- No multiplicity adjustment is applied across the many tests in this document.
- Full-sample numbers are descriptive only.
- The θ̂ formula has F-status: it was checked against two secondary descriptions, and the Ferro–Segers paper was not read.
- Market-wide halt days keep their official closes (D27 item 3).
